A little about me

Clear ideas. Human objects.

I’m an industrial designer drawn to the small decisions that make an object intuitive. I study how people move, carry, gather, and make, then translate those observations into forms that feel clear and considered. Material, color, and a little playfulness help each idea become something people want to keep close. For me, good design is the result of careful research, honest constraints, repeated prototypes, and the patience to remove anything that does not support the experience.

My design philosophy

Strong products begin with empathy and end with restraint. I look for the honest idea at the center of a problem, then use form, color, and material to make that idea easy to understand.

Interests and hobbies

Beyond product design, I’m drawn to painting, visual systems, outdoor experiences, and the way color can give everyday objects a distinct point of view. These experiments keep my eye curious and often find their way back into product work.
